SPECIFICATION GUIDE
Design standards, spatial parameters, and UI abstractions for creating editorial software architecture diagrams and case study illustrations.
1 Core Philosophy: The “Living Blueprint”

Illustrations in the Keizersgracht Media visual universe are never generic SaaS doodles, clip art, or glossy 3D renders. They represent an abstracted architectural blueprint of software:

  • Tangible & Honest: Visually communicate the exact logic of modern software—inputs, data states, validation gates, and human review steps.
  • Minimalist UI Abstraction: Interface states are suggested via skeleton bars, floating cards, status chips, and data tokens—never literal full-screen browser chrome or low-res app screenshots.
  • Warm Editorial Craft: Grounded in warm paper tones, technical hairline strokes, and tactile micro-chamfers rather than synthetic cold tech gradients.
2 The 3-Act Narrative Structure

Every diagram must tell an immediate visual story flowing from left to right:

Act 1: Entry
Raw Input
Unstructured data, customer requests, floor plans, sensor streams.
→
Act 2: Studio Craft
Rules & Processing
AI complexity analysis, logic engine, verified database validation.
→
Act 3: Outcome
Tangible Output
Fixed project price (€), instant human-approved draft, verified sanitation score.
3 Spatial System & Canvas Dimensions

All illustrations adhere to a responsive mathematical coordinate grid:

Desktop Master Canvas

960px × 480px (strict 2:1 aspect ratio). Encapsulated in <ScaledCanvas width={960} height={480}> for seamless vector downscaling on tablets and laptops.

Mobile Stacking Logic

On viewports below 768px, horizontal flows automatically stack vertically, transitioning connectors to downward paths (<DownArrow />).

• Breathing Margins: Maintain at least 48px interior padding from canvas boundaries.
• Node Spacing: Spacing between primary nodes is fixed between 32px and 48px horizontally.

4 Color Palette & Semantic Roles

Colors carry functional meaning. Never apply colors decoratively outside their established roles:

Sand
 #ECECDC Secondary container fill, container border strokes, inactive UI nodes.
Espresso
 #351E1C Primary typography, high-contrast headline data bars, and structural icons.

 #733635 Secondary notes, technical captions, logic rules, and connector labels.

 #FF6037 The Action Accent: Ready states, active step pill, approved draft badges. Strict limit: 1–2 spots per illustration.

 #A0C9CB The Machine / AI State: Algorithmic parsing chips, AI analysis highlights, clean water/data pipeline states.
5 Geometry & The Architectural Chamfer

To avoid generic modern SaaS roundness ("AI slop"), corners use precise micro-radii:

Container Chamfer rounded-[3px]

Outer cards, floating windows, and major diagram panes use 3px radius. Clean, structural, and tactile.

Tag: rounded-[2px] Action: rounded-[2px]
Line Weights & Elevation

• Card Borders: 1.5px solid #ECECDC (Sand stroke).
• Step Dividers: 1px border-t border-border-subtle/55.
• Warm Shadow: shadow-card (0 10px 24px rgba(115, 54, 53, 0.08)). Faint terracotta tint lifts cards off background.

Chamfer Micro-Rules:

• Outer cards & panes: rounded-[3px]  |  Inner chips & status badges: rounded-[2px]  |  Skeleton bars: rounded-[1px].
• Never use circular pills (rounded-full) or large radii (12px+).

8 Do’s and Don’ts Production Checklist
✓ DO
  • Keep generous whitespace: Let the blueprint breathe with wide internal margins.
  • 3-Second Clarity: A viewer should understand input and output in under 3 seconds.
  • Strict Coral Discipline: Reserve coral exclusively for final action / outcome.
  • Architectural Chamfers: Always use rounded-[2px] or rounded-[3px].
✗ DON’T
  • No 3D or Isometric Art: Avoid bubbly cartoon characters or 3D plastic cubes.
  • No Neon Gradients: Never introduce purple, bright indigo, or generic AI gradients.
  • No Full Browser Dumps: Do not paste full screenshots; abstract the essential data.
  • No Rounded-Full Pills: Steer clear of circular lozenges (16px+ radius).
