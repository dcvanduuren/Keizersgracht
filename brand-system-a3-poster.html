00 GUIDING PRINCIPLE

These guidelines are a compass, not a rulebook. We build software, AI agents, and data tools around how teams actually work. Our visual identity reflects that: grounded, unpretentious, and human. Whether designing a website, an internal tool, or physical collateral, keep it clear, tactile, and free of agency fluff.

01 BRAND AND VOICE
  • Two senior operators. Direct communication and real flexibility. No account managers, no six-month slide-deck phases, no corporate overhead.
  • Speak like a person. Be direct, warm, and conversational. Write like you speak over coffee.
  • Active outcomes. Lead with what the software accomplishes ("Turning incoming messages into ready-to-send replies"). Avoid speculative tech jargon.
  • No synthetic hype. Ban fake percentage badges ("+340% ROI"), made-up latency counters, and synthetic ISO badges. Real utility needs no hype.
  • Human in the loop. When presenting automation, always show the human review, verification, and approval step before action executes.
The sweet spot

Replace inflated marketing claims with simple, grounded sentences:

  • "Software that organizes incoming orders and drafts replies." (Not: "Enterprise cognitive AI synergy platform").
  • "Tools built around the way your team actually works." (Not: "Revolutionizing human workflows via neural pipelines").
Visual anti-patterns
  • No generic AI tropes. Never use glowing neon orbs, floating plastic cubes, or purple/indigo gradients.
  • No cartoon people. Show human teams through the tools they use or the data they inspect.
  • No bloated bentos. Avoid decorative card mosaics packed with meaningless icon filler; let actual tasks dictate layout.
02 TYPOGRAPHY AND HIERARCHY

A clear three-tier system: geometric Dutch humanist display for structure, neutral geometric sans for reading, and crisp monospace for technical values.

  • Display — Sintony Bold (700): Architectural geometric sans with Dutch humanist weight. Reserved for hero statements, H1/H2 titles, and focal numbers.
  • Body & UI — Poppins (400, 500, 600): Open geometric sans with high legibility across screens and print. Regular for body, Medium for subtitles, SemiBold for UI controls.
  • Metadata — JetBrains Mono (500): Crisp monospace for technical tags, hex values, tracking stats, and micro-labels.

Typesetting rules
  • Sentence case: Use natural sentence case for all headlines, subheads, and buttons. Avoid aggressive Title Casing.
  • Tracked caps: Eyebrows and micro-tags always use uppercase with tracking set between +0.12em and +0.18em.
  • Tabular numbers: In UI dashboards, tables, and metric displays, always enable font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.
  • Line measure: Keep running editorial text between 45 and 70 characters per line for comfortable cadence.
  • Container breathing room: Never squeeze text against borders. Always size cards with enough vertical padding so trailing lines sit naturally.

Material honesty & print stock
  • Digital interfaces: Should feel like heavyweight matte paper, not glossy plastic. Avoid synthetic glassmorphism and specular glows.
  • Print collateral: In stationery, cards, and packaging, specify 450–600gsm uncoated warm cotton stock (FSC-certified). Blind deboss or spot terracotta ink.
  • Contrast: Espresso on Canvas achieves 14.2:1 (AAA). Terracotta on Canvas achieves 7.1:1 (AAA). Coral is reserved for interactive actions.
04 CORNER RADII AND SYSTEM

We reject rounded-full pill shapes and generic 20px bubble cards. Keizersgracht relies on an architectural micro-radius:

✔ APPROVED SPEC
2px – 3px radius
Architectural micro-chamfer
✖ FORBIDDEN
16px+ / Pill radius
Generic SaaS bubble shape
  • Cards: 3px radius, with a warm shadow: 0 10px 24px rgba(115, 54, 53, 0.08).
  • Checkboxes, buttons, controls: 2px radius.
  • Skeleton and chart bars: 1px radius.
  • Never use fully rounded pill shapes.
Spatial 8-point rhythm

All layout margins, card padding, and flex gaps derive strictly from multiples of 8px (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px).

Photography & visual media
  • Documentary realism: Real founders, physical team spaces, and operational workflows only. Strict rectangular crops with zero rounded corners and zero vignette fade.
  • Warm mineral grade: Layered with a 3% warm Coral tint (rgba(255, 96, 55, 0.03)) and a hairline border (rgba(53, 30, 28, 0.08)).
Universal collateral rules
  • Cross-media consistency: Whether designing an internal dashboard, client portal, or physical letterhead, adhere to the 3px micro-chamfer and 8px rhythm.
  • Less, but tactile: White space is functional breathing room, not empty void. Let content sit comfortably on the canvas.
